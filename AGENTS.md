# 二战德国平民史调研项目

Always converse in Chinese. Code comments in English.

This project produces a series of standalone HTML research reports on German civilian life during WWII (1933–1945): survival, daily routines, social psychology, and the racialized boundaries of “normal life.” Scholarly, critical, archival. Follow the skill at `.cursor/skills/ww2-german-research/SKILL.md` for each cycle.

Stance: objective narration of German civilian experience. Prefer archives, peer-reviewed work, museums, and quality journalism. Do not romanticize the dictatorship. Do not caricature ordinary people. Civilian suffering and Nazi crimes belong in the same sentence.
